# Vehicle tariff artwork — 2026-09-06

Created with the built-in ImageGen tool for the SmartTaxi screen redesign. Two separate generations; original prompts below. Reference screenshots informed the photographic direction, but no vehicle was cut out of a third-party reference. These are illustrative tariff-category images, not a promise of the assigned vehicle's make/model.

Both images are 1536 × 1024 transparent PNGs. The same files are used by Flutter and React web. Existing artwork has been retained; active Economy/Delivery paths and pubspec entries now use these files. No invented Comfort/Business tariff was introduced.

## Economy prompt

Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: transparent raster vehicle cutout for a premium blue/white taxi app tariff row, displayed at 90px wide. Primary request: a single photorealistic modern white four-door economical sedan, original generic brand-neutral design, real full-size automotive proportions. Composition: front-left three-quarter view, nose facing left, camera only slightly above headlamp height, complete car including wheels, fills about 92% of a wide 3:2 landscape canvas. Clean studio lighting, realistic pearl white paint, charcoal glass, modest silver alloy wheels. Genuinely transparent background with alpha, tiny soft contact shadow only. No background scene, floor, people, props, taxi roof sign, text, watermark, logos, emblems, license-plate lettering or recognizable branded grille. Not a toy, not cartoon, not exaggerated SUV or sports car. Make a crisp natural catalog cutout, for a real application, not an interface mockup.

## Delivery prompt

Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: transparent raster vehicle cutout for a premium blue/white taxi app delivery tariff row, displayed at 90px wide. Primary request: a single photorealistic compact white urban delivery panel van, original generic brand-neutral design, real full-size automotive proportions, short wheelbase small courier van with two front seats and enclosed cargo sides. Composition: front-left three-quarter view, nose facing left, camera only slightly above headlamp height, complete vehicle including wheels, fills about 92% of a wide 3:2 landscape canvas. Clean studio lighting, realistic pearl white paint, charcoal glass, modest silver wheels. Genuinely transparent background with alpha, tiny soft contact shadow only. No background scene, floor, people, parcels outside vehicle, taxi roof sign, text, watermark, logos, emblems, license-plate lettering or recognizable branded grille. Not a toy, not cartoon, not exaggerated wheels, not large freight truck, not sedan. Make a crisp natural catalog cutout matching restrained photographic passenger sedan assets, not an interface mockup.

## Verification

Both outputs inspected directly, transparent alpha checked, then viewed in the actual 360px web tariff screen and physical Android tariff screen. The delivery asset depicts a compact cargo van, not a second passenger sedan. No generated interface mockup is used as QA evidence.
